Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Decrave ("the app," "we," "us") is a wellness tool that helps people beat nicotine cravings without waiting for a formal quit date. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what choices you have.

1. What we collect

Decrave is built to keep as much as possible on your device:

  • Craving logs — timestamp, outcome (beaten or smoked), trigger, intensity. Stored locally on your device via Apple's SwiftData framework.
  • Reduction plan — your daily cigarette target and quit date, if you choose to set one.
  • Settings — things like your price-per-pack estimate and notification preference, stored locally via iOS's standard app-storage mechanism.
  • Anonymous product analytics — via TelemetryDeck, which does not collect personal identifiers, IP addresses, or advertising identifiers. We use it only to understand which features are used, not who is using them.

Features like Trigger Radar, Milestones, and the Insights charts (craving intensity, top triggers, money trajectory, peak hours) don't collect anything new — they're calculated entirely on your device from the craving logs above, and that calculation never leaves your phone.

2. iCloud sync

If you're signed into iCloud, Decrave can sync your craving logs and reduction plan across your own devices using Apple's CloudKit private database. This data is stored in the private portion of your personal iCloud account — we do not have access to it, and it is never shared with other users or with us. If you're not signed into iCloud, the app works entirely offline with no loss of functionality.

3. What we don't do

  • We don't use advertising SDKs or ad-tracking identifiers.
  • We don't sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We don't read your messages, contacts, location, or any other on-device data outside what you explicitly log in the app.
  • We don't require an account or login — there's nothing tied to your identity on our servers, because we don't operate servers that store your personal logs.

4. Subscriptions

Decrave Pro subscriptions are handled entirely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your payment details — that's handled by Apple under its own privacy policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Settings app.

5. Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, it's scheduled locally on your device. We don't use a push notification server, so we never see when or whether you received it.

6. Your choices

  • Export — Decrave Pro lets you export everything you've logged as a CSV file at any time, for a date range you choose.
  • Delete — uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. If iCloud sync was on, you can also delete the app's data from your iCloud account via Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone.

7. Children's privacy

Decrave is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your country).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is handled, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, notify you in the app.

9. Contact
