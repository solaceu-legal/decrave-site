Not another day counter

Streaks punish a slip. Decrave doesn't.

Most quit apps ask when you quit and count the days since. One slip and the counter drops to zero — right when you need it most.

The old way

Day 1, Day 2, Day 0.

Streak counters only move in one direction: down on a slip. When a bad day becomes "back to Day 0," staying quit starts to feel pointless — so people quit the app instead of the cigarettes.

The Decrave way

A count that only goes up.

Decrave counts how many cravings you've beaten — total, ever. That number never resets. If you smoke, it doesn't move backward. It just waits for the next craving you beat.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to set a quit date?
No. Decrave never asks you to commit to a quit date up front, and everything works without one. If you set a daily goal and hit it consistently for two weeks, Decrave suggests picking a quit day — but only as a suggestion you can accept or skip.
I logged that I smoked. Did I lose my progress?
No. Your cravings-beaten count and Momentum never reset to zero, no matter what you log. Decrave tracks how many cravings you've beaten in total, not a streak of days — logging a slip adds one more data point, it doesn't erase the ones before it.
What stays free?
The counter, SOS tools (urge surfing, breathing, and redirection), logging, daily actions, your reduction plan, personal SOS suggestions, headline stats, and faster system entry points are free forever. Where it really matters — in the middle of a craving — you'll never hit a paywall. Pro unlocks deeper reports, Trigger Radar, peak hours, smart reminders, and data export.
How quickly can I open SOS?
SOS is the center tab in Decrave. In version 1.1, you can also open it from supported Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, Control Center, and App Shortcuts, so the same flow is easier to reach when a craving hits.
Does Decrave sync across my devices?
Yes, if you're signed in to iCloud — your logs sync privately through your own iCloud account, and we never see that data. Not signed in to iCloud? The app still works fully on a single device.
How do I cancel my Decrave Pro subscription?
Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely through Apple, not us. On your iPhone, go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Decrave, and you can view, change, or cancel from there.
Does Decrave replace professional support?
No. Decrave is a wellness and habit-tracking tool. It does not provide health advice or replace support from a qualified professional. If you have questions about nicotine dependence or cessation, talk to a doctor or a licensed program.
