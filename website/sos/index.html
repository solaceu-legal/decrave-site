About this exercise

How the one-minute craving exercise works

Decrave SOS offers four short prompts over one minute. You can use the written steps below on their own, or select “Try one minute” for the guided timer.

  1. Notice the urge. Acknowledge that you want a cigarette without deciding what to do immediately.
  2. Notice where you feel it. Pay attention to a sensation in your hands, chest, or mouth. There is no right answer.
  3. Make room for the moment. Keep your breathing comfortable and notice whether the feeling changes or stays the same.
  4. Choose your next move. Bring your attention back to your surroundings and choose one manageable action.

You can pause, continue, or finish the timer whenever you want. It also pauses if you switch to another browser tab or app.

What if I still want to smoke afterward?

The minute is a pause, not a promise that a craving will disappear. You could move away from a smoking cue, drink water, take a short walk, or contact someone supportive. Our cigarette craving guide explains more options and links to the public health resources behind that guidance.

Do I need the Decrave app?

No. This exercise is free to use on the web, with no account or personal details to enter. The iPhone app offers additional SOS tools and craving logging; the web exercise does not save a personal history.
